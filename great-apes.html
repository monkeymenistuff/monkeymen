BEYOND MONKEYS

What Makes An Ape?

Great apes are some of the most intelligent and socially complex animals on Earth.

🧠 Intelligence & Social Life

Great apes are known for their remarkable intelligence. They can use tools, solve problems, communicate with one another and learn from their surroundings.

Unlike monkeys, great apes generally do not have tails. They also tend to have larger brains relative to their body size and highly flexible shoulders and arms.

Great apes include orangutans, gorillas, chimpanzees and bonobos — as well as humans.

GREAT APE SPECIES

The Great Apes

Each great ape has its own unique personality, habitat and way of life.

🦧

Orangutan

Pongo

Orangutans are highly intelligent apes native to the rainforests of Borneo and Sumatra. They spend much of their lives in trees and are famous for their reddish-orange hair.

🦍

Gorilla

Gorilla

Gorillas are the largest living primates. Despite their impressive size and strength, they are generally peaceful, social animals that live in family groups.

🐵

Chimpanzee

Pan troglodytes

Chimpanzees are extremely intelligent and closely related to humans. They use tools, form complex social relationships and can learn from one another.

🐒

Bonobo

Pan paniscus

Bonobos are found in the forests of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. They are highly social and are particularly known for their peaceful social behavior.

🧑

Human

Homo sapiens

Humans are also great apes. We share a common evolutionary history with the other great apes and are especially known for language, technology and complex culture.

DID YOU KNOW?

Ape vs Monkey

They may look similar, but there are some important differences.

🐒 Monkeys

Many monkeys have tails, although there are exceptions. Monkeys are generally smaller than great apes and belong to several different groups of primates.

🦍 Great Apes

Great apes do not have tails. They generally have larger brains, more flexible shoulders and complex social behavior.

🧠 Intelligence

Both monkeys and apes can be highly intelligent, but great apes are especially well known for advanced problem-solving, tool use and learning.

🌳 Life In The Trees

Some great apes, particularly orangutans, spend a huge amount of time in trees. Others, such as gorillas, spend much more time on the ground.
