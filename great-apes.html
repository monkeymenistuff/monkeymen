KNOW THE DIFFERENCE

Monkey vs Great Ape

They are both primates, but there are several important differences.

Feature Monkeys Great Apes
Tail Many species have tails No external tail
Body Shape Generally more varied Broad chests and flexible shoulders
Intelligence Many species are highly intelligent Exceptional problem-solving abilities
Movement Varies greatly by species Extremely flexible shoulders and arms
Examples Capuchins, macaques, baboons Gorillas, chimpanzees, orangutans

PRIMATE SCIENCE

What Makes An Ape?

Great apes share several characteristics that distinguish them from monkeys.

🦴 No External Tail

One of the easiest ways to distinguish great apes from many monkeys is the absence of an external tail. Great apes have a tailbone internally, but no visible tail.

🧠 Large Brains

Great apes have relatively large brains and demonstrate sophisticated behaviours including learning, communication, memory and problem-solving.

🤸 Flexible Shoulders

Their shoulder structure allows great apes to move their arms through a wide range of motion. This is especially useful for climbing and moving through trees.

👥 Complex Societies

Many great apes live in complex social groups. They can form long-lasting relationships, learn from one another and cooperate with members of their group.
