THE JUNGLE

Meet The Monkeys

From tiny tamarins to powerful mandrills, monkeys come in an incredible variety of shapes, sizes and personalities.

🐒

Capuchin

Clever, curious monkeys famous for their impressive problem-solving abilities.

🕷️

Spider Monkey

Incredible climbers with long limbs and powerful gripping tails.

📣

Howler Monkey

Their enormous calls can travel surprisingly far through tropical forests.

🐵

Squirrel Monkey

Tiny, energetic and highly social monkeys that live in groups.

🌴

Proboscis Monkey

Famous for their enormous noses and their preference for swampy habitats.

👑

Golden Lion Tamarin

Small Brazilian monkeys with spectacular golden-orange fur.

🏔️

Gelada

Highly social Ethiopian primates known for living in large groups.

♨️

Japanese Macaque

Also called snow monkeys, they are famous for enjoying hot springs.

🦁

Mandrill

One of the world's most colourful mammals, with a spectacular face.

🌿

Vervet Monkey

Clever African monkeys with surprisingly complex communication.
