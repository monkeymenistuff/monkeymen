Meet the Monkeys

From tiny tamarins to powerful mandrills, the monkey world is ridiculously diverse.

🐒

Capuchin

Highly intelligent and curious monkeys famous for their problem-solving abilities.

🕷️

Spider Monkey

Amazing climbers that use their long limbs and powerful tails to move through forests.

📣

Howler Monkey

Famous for incredibly loud calls that can travel for miles through the rainforest.

🐵

Squirrel Monkey

Small, energetic and social monkeys that spend much of their lives in large groups.

🌴

Proboscis Monkey

Famous for their enormous noses and love of swampy forests and mangroves.

👑

Golden Lion Tamarin

Tiny Brazilian monkeys recognized by their spectacular golden-orange fur.

🏔️

Gelada

Social Ethiopian primates that spend much of their time living in huge groups.

♨️

Japanese Macaque

Also called snow monkeys, they're famous for relaxing in hot springs during winter.

🦁

Mandrill

One of the world's most colorful mammals, known for its bright blue and red face.

🌿

Vervet Monkey

Clever African monkeys with surprisingly complex communication systems.
