Meet the monkeys.

Capuchin

Capuchins are intelligent New World monkeys known for curiosity, dexterity and problem-solving.

🌳

Spider Monkey

Spider monkeys have long limbs and prehensile tails that help them navigate forest canopies.

📣

Howler Monkey

Howler monkeys are famous for powerful calls that can carry long distances through forests.

🌿

Squirrel Monkey

Small and energetic, squirrel monkeys often move through trees in lively social groups.

🌴

Proboscis Monkey

Proboscis monkeys are known for their distinctive noses and their association with wetland forests.

🏔️

Gelada

Geladas are grass-eating primates associated with highland environments.

👑

Tamarin

Tamarins are tiny primates with distinctive appearances and highly social lifestyles.

🐵

Macaque

Macaques are a diverse group of Old World monkeys found across parts of Asia and Africa.

Where They Live

From rainforest to mountains.

Different monkey species have adapted to dramatically different environments.

🌴 Rainforest

Dense forests provide food, shelter and a huge network of branches for tree-dwelling primates.

🏔️ Highlands

Some primates are adapted to cooler high-elevation environments.

🌊 Wetlands

Rivers, mangroves and swamp forests support unique monkey communities.

🌾 Grasslands

Some primates spend significant amounts of time feeding on open grassland environments.
